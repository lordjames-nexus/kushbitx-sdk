# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues.

Email **Admin@kushbitx.com** with:

- the affected service or endpoint;
- clear reproduction steps;
- the expected and observed behavior; and
- the potential security impact.

Do not include private keys, seed phrases, wallet credentials, payment signatures, or other secrets. KushBitx will acknowledge actionable reports and coordinate remediation and disclosure when appropriate.
